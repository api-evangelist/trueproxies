SOCKS5 on TrueProxies now carries UDP to ports 443 and 53, so QUIC, HTTP/3 and DNS go through the same proxy as your TCP traffic. It is included on every paid Unlimited Bandwidth plan and traffic pack, with the same host, port and login.
