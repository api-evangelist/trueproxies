# TrueProxies

> Residential and datacenter proxy service with 50M+ ethically sourced residential IPs across 150+ countries.

TrueProxies provides proxy infrastructure for teams that need reliable, geo-targeted internet access. Unlimited Residential IPv4 supports country targeting; GB-Based additionally supports city, region, and ASN targeting.

## Products

### Residential IPv4 — Unlimited Bandwidth
- URL: https://trueproxies.com/proxies/unlimited-residential-proxies/
- Pricing: Starts from $4.08/hour for 10 Mbps. Hourly, daily, weekly, monthly plans.
- Unlimited bandwidth at your selected speed tier. A separate 1.3M+ worldwide pool with rotating or sticky residential IPs and country targeting.

### Residential IPv4 — GB-Based
- URL: https://trueproxies.com/proxies/residential-ipv4-gb/
- Pricing: From $1.64/GB.
- A separate 50M+ pool across 150+ countries, with country, city, region, and ASN targeting. Pay only for data transferred. Monthly plans.

### Residential IPv6
- URL: https://trueproxies.com/proxies/residential-ipv6/
- Status: Coming soon
- Availability: Coming soon. Join the existing waitlist for launch updates.
- Planned service: rotating IPv6 addresses on a US ISP network. Transfer allowances and any fair-use limits are not confirmed yet.

### Datacenter IPv6
- URL: https://trueproxies.com/proxies/datacenter-ipv6/
- Pricing: From $3.99/day at 25 Mbps. Daily, 7-day, and monthly plans up to 400 Mbps.
- IPv6-only. A private /48 per included location, rotation on each new connection, sticky sessions by token, no per-GB charge, and a published fair-use limit per plan.

## Key Features
- 50M+ ethically sourced residential IPs
- Unlimited supports worldwide country targeting; GB-Based covers 150+ countries with country, city, region, and ASN targeting
- HTTP, HTTPS, and SOCKS5 protocols
- Residential IPv4 supports sticky or rotating sessions; Residential IPv6 is rotating-only; Datacenter IPv6 rotates by default and supports sticky sessions
- IP whitelist or username/password authentication
- Published Residential IPv4 and Datacenter IPv6 plans do not bill concurrency as a separate item; effective capacity still depends on the plan and workload
- Residential IPv4 publishes 10 Mbps–1 Gbps unlimited tiers and GB-Based usage plans
- Support through the published contact channels
- Free trials are product-specific and start from the canonical trial page
